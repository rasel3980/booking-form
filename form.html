Driving School Policies and Regulations

1. Student Requirements

  • Eligibility: Students must be at least 15½ years old. A valid learner's permit is required before behind-the-wheel training.
  • Documentation: Valid learner's permit and ID required before each lesson.
  • Health and Safety: Students must be free of drugs or alcohol. Suspected impairment results in immediate cancellation.

2. Instructor Requirements

  • Licensing: All instructors are DMV-certified and background-checked.
  • Conduct: Instructors must maintain professional behavior and adhere to driving laws.
  • Training: Periodic training to stay current with state laws.

3. Scheduling and Cancellations

  • Booking: First-come, first-served. Confirmed at least 24 hours before the lesson.
  • Cancellation: 48 hours notice required to avoid fees.
  • No-Show: Forfeits lesson and may incur a no-show fee.

4. Payments and Refunds

  • Payment: Full payment at booking unless otherwise arranged.
  • Refunds: Only under specific conditions; cancellations may incur fees.
  • Packages: Discounts available; unused lessons may not be refunded after a period.

5. Use of School Property

  • Vehicles: Students responsible for damages from reckless behavior.
  • Materials: Must be returned in good condition.
  • Dress Code: Closed-toe shoes and appropriate clothing required.

6. Safety and Conduct

  • Seatbelts: Required at all times.
  • Cell Phones: Prohibited during lessons.
  • Behavior: Respectful behavior required at all times.

7. Termination of Lessons

  • School's Right: May terminate for repeated violations or dangerous behavior.
  • Student's Right: May terminate enrollment subject to refund policy.

8. Compliance with California DMV

  • Laws: All lessons comply with California DMV guidelines.
  • Testing: School prepares students but does not guarantee passing.

9. Privacy and Confidentiality

  • Data: Personal information kept confidential.
  • Recordings: Not permitted without consent.

10. Terms And Condition

  • Cancellations less than 24 hours before appointment incur a $75.00 fee.

11. Freeway Driving Terms

  • Freeway driving introduced only when instructor determines student is ready.

Rules may be customized to align with California DMV regulations.
